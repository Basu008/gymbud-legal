Account Deletion

GymBud allows users to delete their account directly from within the app. When you delete your account, your personal information and associated workout data will be deleted or anonymized from our systems, unless retention is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

If you are unable to delete your account from the app, email us at: basu.singh05@gmail.com.
