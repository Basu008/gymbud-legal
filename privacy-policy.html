GymBud Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

GymBud is a fitness and workout tracking app that helps users create routines, log workouts, and track progress.

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Information We Collect

GymBud may collect information such as your name, email address, username, profile details, height, weight, workout routines, workout logs, exercise data, progress records, and app usage information.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to create and manage your account, store your workout data, show your progress, improve the app, maintain app security, fix bugs, and provide support.

Health and Fitness Data

GymBud may store fitness-related information such as workouts, exercises, weight, height, sets, reps, and progress. This information is used only to provide app functionality and improve your fitness tracking experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, analytics, database, or support providers.

Data Retention

We keep your information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app’s services. When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymize your personal information unless we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Account and Data Deletion

GymBud allows users to delete their account directly from within the app. When you delete your account, your personal information and associated workout data will be deleted or anonymized from our systems, unless retention is required for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Children’s Privacy

GymBud is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
